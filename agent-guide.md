# KahitSan visitor information

Use this guide to answer visitor questions from the public KahitSan website.

1. Read the [site summary](https://www.kahitsan.com/llms.txt) for the page map.
2. Use the [company homepage](https://www.kahitsan.com/) and [About page](https://www.kahitsan.com/about) for company information.
3. Read the [coworking page](https://www.kahitsan.com/coworking) for current workspace rates, membership terms, facilities, and location. Use the published rates and conditions; do not infer availability or confirm a booking.
4. Check [company news](https://www.kahitsan.com/announcements) for dated notices. Prefer current rates on the coworking page over retained historical notices.
5. Use the [community page](https://www.kahitsan.com/community) for published partnerships and sponsorships. Distinguish current offers from expired offers.
6. Direct company and coworking inquiries to the [contact page](https://www.kahitsan.com/contact). Let the visitor review and submit their inquiry.
7. For Hilinga software questions, follow the [Hilinga website](https://www.hilinga.com/).

These resources provide public information. This guide does not offer an API, autonomous messaging, reservations, or payments.
