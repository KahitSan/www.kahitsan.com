Dear Partners and Community,

After careful consideration and planning since early October, we are announcing a pricing update for KahitSan Coworking, effective November 1, 2025.

We encountered challenges that taught us the importance of continuous improvement. Intermittent WiFi disruptions, weather control issues, and facility wear affected the customer experience. While we resolved those core issues, we recognized that the workspace still needed upgraded blinds, better partitions, expanded amenities, and other improvements.

Our progress was limited by available resources. This pricing adjustment was intended to help fund facility improvements and strengthen KahitSan Coworking for long-term operation.

We aimed to balance sustainability with affordability so the workspace could remain accessible to students, professionals, and community organizations.

## Why the pricing changed

### Facility investment

Additional revenue was intended to support repairs, workspace upgrades, and expanded amenities.

### Accessible, sustainable operation

The rates were designed to keep the space available to the community while supporting reliable long-term operation.

### Ongoing review

The announcement noted that rates could change as capacity, operating costs, and services evolved.
